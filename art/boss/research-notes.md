# Giant boss design and motion references

These sources informed an original procedural actor; no source character model, image, animation, or other external asset is included in the game.

- **Visual mass and scale — [Shadow of the Colossus art gallery, Creative Uncut](https://www.creativeuncut.com/art_shadow-of-the-colossus_a.html).** The page credits artists Shunpei Suzuki and Hitoshi Niwa and lists Colossus renders, line-up art, and sketches. Used as a reference for a large, readable humanoid silhouette against a smaller environment, not copied as a design.
- **Giant motion — [5 Rules For Animating Colossi, GameAnim](https://www.gameanim.com/2021/09/30/5-rules-for-animating-colossi/).** The article summarizes Masanobu Tanaka's CEDEC 2021 talk: large mass starts smoothly; head and arms should feel heavy; avoid excessive overlap; retain restrained high-frequency impact detail. This informed the boss's low cadence, slow pursuit, and restrained limb swing.
- **Heavy gait — [The Biomechanical Characteristics of the Strongman Yoke Walk, Frontiers in Sports and Active Living](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sports-and-active-living/articles/10.3389/fspor.2021.670297/full).** The study reports hip flexion and slight-to-neutral knee flexion at heel strike, reduced stride length/rate/mean velocity in the initial interval, and increased stance duration. This is a real human loaded-walk reference, not direct evidence about fictional giants; it informed the boss's slower cadence and heavier stance.
- **Camera and scale framing — [The Animation of Shadow of the Colossus: The Camera, PlayFrame](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdBMA-3Crng).** A frame-by-frame companion source about camera presentation in the game. Used as contextual framing reference only; not copied.


## Shadow-body revision reference pass (2026-10-29)

- **Visual — [Michelangelo's David, right view 2, Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Michelangelo%27s_David_-_right_view_2.jpg).** The documented 1501–1504 white Carrara-marble sculpture was checked for broad athletic proportions and a light finish. The game boss reuses its own `src/hero.json` Shadow mesh; no photo or third-party model is included. The Commons page lists CC BY-SA 4.0 and a separate Italian cultural-heritage reuse notice, so the image itself is reference-only.
- **Human motion — [The Biomechanical Characteristics of the Strongman Yoke Walk, Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sports-and-active-living/articles/10.3389/fspor.2021.670297/full).** The study measured 19 athletes carrying a loaded yoke and reports short strides with relatively long stance duration. This is contextual real-human gait evidence, not direct evidence about fictional giants; the existing boss's restrained cadence remains appropriate.
- **Large-character motion — [5 Rules For Animating Colossi, GameAnim](https://www.gameanim.com/2021/09/30/5-rules-for-animating-colossi/).** The summary of Masanobu Tanaka's CEDEC talk informed smooth starts and restrained heavy-limb overlap. No source artwork, animation, or code was copied.
